Amount-private by design

Everyone else sees ciphertext.

Every Solana payment is normally public: the amount, your balance, your whole history. Envelope keeps the transfer on Solana but encrypts the numbers, so a block explorer shows that a payment happened and nothing about how much.

What you can do

Everything a wallet does, without the audience.

Private dollars and SKR

USDC and SKR go in one to one and live on Solana as encrypted balances that only your phone can read.

Pay one person or twenty

Send to an address or a QR code. A batch to several people takes one approval, and each sees only their own amount.

Pots for group gifts

A wedding, a farewell, a birthday. Guests chip in privately, the host sees the total, and nobody sees what anyone else gave.

Gift links

Send money to someone who isn't on Envelope yet. They open the link, install the app, and claim it into their own private balance.

No SOL, ever

Opened by your fingerprint

Runs on older phones

Works on Android WebViews back to Chrome 85, including Huawei and other phones without Google services.

Withdraw any time

Turn private dollars or SKR back into regular USDC or SKR in your wallet with one approval.

New

Send money to someone who isn't here yet.

A gift link carries private dollars or SKR to anyone. The amount stays hidden on-chain, and claiming costs them nothing.

  1. Choose an amount and confirm with your fingerprint. You get a link and a QR code.
  2. Send it however you like. Whoever opens it installs Envelope and taps Claim.
  3. The gift lands in their private balance. Until then, you can take it back.

Questions people ask

Can the person I pay see my balance?

They see your wallet address, so they can look up what any wallet shows: your regular USDC, SKR and SOL, and what you moved into or out of Envelope. Your private balance and the amount of each private payment stay sealed. Keeping a balance in Envelope, instead of topping up right before each payment, keeps your activity from being pieced together.

Is Envelope anonymous?

No. It hides amounts, not people. Who paid whom is visible on Solana, as with any wallet. What nobody else can see is how much moved and how much you hold privately.

Do I need SOL?

No. SKR pays the network fees, and a new wallet’s first refill is free, so you can start with an empty wallet. Members’ fees are covered entirely.

What is SKR used for?

Three things: a membership pass for bigger limits and no send fees, the fuel that pays network fees, and a private SKR balance you can hold and send like dollars.

Where are my keys, and what if I lose my phone?

Your encryption keys are made on your phone from one wallet signature and kept behind your fingerprint. They never leave the device. On a new phone, connect the same wallet and Envelope makes the same keys again, and finds your open pots on-chain.

What happens if a gift link gets shared further?

Anyone with the link can claim the gift, so send it only to the person it’s for. Until someone claims it, you can take it back from the gift screen. Once claimed, the link stops working.

Which phones and wallets work?

Android 7 or newer, with any wallet that supports Mobile Wallet Adapter, such as Solflare. It also runs on phones without Google services, like many Huawei models.

Is it on mainnet? Has it been audited?

Envelope runs on Solana devnet today, with test USDC and test SKR. It hasn’t had an external audit yet; that comes before mainnet. The threat model and how we verify it are public.
